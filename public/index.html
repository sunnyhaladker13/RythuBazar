Typical prices across all Rythu Bazars

    Reading the strip: the line runs from the lowest to the highest rate across bazars that reported in the last three days. The square is the typical (middle) rate, or the bazar's own rate when you're viewing one bazar.

    Rates as published by the Telangana Agricultural Marketing Department's Rythu Bazar Information System, in ₹ per kg. A few items such as drumsticks and leafy greens are sold per piece or bunch, and bazars don't say which, so those aren't compared. Bazars report through the morning, usually by 1 PM.
